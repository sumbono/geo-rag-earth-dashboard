## 2. Verdict (Step 3)

**Separate volumes.** The Coolify stack's `pgdata` is a named volume owned by
that compose project; the compose stack's postgres is a different service
with a different volume. Stopping the compose stack's `web`/`api` therefore
cannot touch the Coolify database, and vice versa. **No shared-DB hazard
between web/api stop-list and either postgres.**

Residual unknowns until §4 runs: exact container names, which volume backs
each db in the live engine, and each stack's `JWT_SECRET`/`SECRET_KEY` env.

## 3. Keeper stack (Task 0.3 decision, pre-registered)

**Keeper = Coolify-managed stack** (Option A ruling, 2026-10-10, reconfirmed
by the fix-and-improve-plan): it owns the Traefik router for
`geo.sumbono.dev`, the `hf_cache` volume (avoids a ~578 MB model
re-download), and Coolify's deploy/restart automation. After merge, Coolify
redeploys `main` (11-path API) and only the compose stack's `web`+`api` are
stopped — never any postgres in the same step.
